About me

Hello, I’m Jieru.

I am a master's student in Virtual Reality and Augmented Reality at Lund University, Sweden. I hold a bachelor's degree in Information Science and Engineering from Ritsumeikan University, Japan.

My interests lie at the intersection of extended reality, human perception, computer vision, and human-centered AI. I am particularly interested in how intelligent systems can interpret physical context and present information through intuitive, spatial interaction.

I enjoy building research prototypes that connect technical implementation with human experience, from XR monitoring interfaces to multimodal accessibility tools.
