03 / Background

A little context.

From visual information
to lived interaction.

I’m Jieru Dong, currently studying Virtual Reality and Augmented Reality at Lund University. My background in Information Science and Engineering and my ongoing work in XR have shaped an interest in how technical systems are perceived, understood, and used.

The website is deliberately structured as a readable research archive. This prototype uses abstract visual diagrams for art direction; the finished version will use genuine project documentation and clearly distinguish research in progress from published work.
