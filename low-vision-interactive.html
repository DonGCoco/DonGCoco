02 / System pipeline

See. Describe. Speak.

One of the thesis's important design questions was that a user with low vision might not know whether an image is sufficiently sharp to support visual understanding. The system therefore integrates image restoration before visual description and speech interaction.

03 / Contribution & evidence

From an assumption
to an experiment.

Individual bachelor’s thesis, Ritsumeikan University, January 2025. I framed the shopping-information problem, prepared paired image data, integrated the restoration, vision-language and speech components, and evaluated image quality and description overlap.

The initial concept assumed that users could provide a usable photograph. I recognised that I could judge blur and retake a photo because I could see it, while the intended users might not be able to do so. This motivated investigating restoration before visual interpretation.

Selected results reported in the thesis, not a new benchmark run.
